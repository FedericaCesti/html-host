01Mon Aug 3
2h drivejumbo stopdinner
Arrival
Land at Heraklion and pick up the rental car.
Jumbo (Heraklion)
Stop at Jumbo on 62 Martyron Avenue. Open Mon–Sat 9–21.
Drive West
Highway to Chania (~2 hours). Scenic north-coast drive with mountain views.
Late Afternoon
Check in and walk Chania's Venetian Harbor: the Egyptian Lighthouse, waterfront, Old Town lanes (Zambeliou and Theotokopoulou streets).
Dinner
Tamam: Persian-Greek fusion in the Old Town. Book ahead.
02Tue Aug 4
hidden coveseafood dinnerold town
Morning
Drive 20 min to Seitan Limania on the Akrotiri Peninsula. Turquoise cove between steep cliffs. 15–20 min hike down. Arrive before 9 AM.
Afternoon
Explore the Agora (Municipal Market), Stivanadika street, the Mosque of the Janissaries. Lunch at Kapari: classic taverna, daily specials.
Sunset
Drinks at Pallas rooftop.
Dinner
Thalassino Ageri: waterfront seafood taverna. Book ahead.
03Wed Aug 5
knossos2h to heraklionmuseum
Timing: Flight lands ~12:00. Leave Chania ~10 AM.
10:00 AM - Depart Chania
Relaxed morning. Coffee in the Old Town, then hit the highway east (~2 hours to Heraklion).
~12:00 - Airport Pickup
Collect your friend at Heraklion Airport. The airport is small and easy to navigate.
12:30 PM - Lunch in Heraklion
Head to Lions Square or 1866 Market Street for lunch. Try bougatsa (custard pastry).
2:30 PM - Archaeological Museum
Original Minoan frescoes (Bull Leaper, Ladies in Blue). Combo ticket available. ~1 hour.
4:00 PM - Knossos Palace
10 min from the museum. Book a 4 PM timed entry slot. 1.5 hours.
~6:00 PM - Drive Back
Drive back to Chania (~2h).
Dinner
Fourogatos: waterfront Cretan cuisine. Book ahead.
04Thu Aug 6
pink sand1h 20m each way
7:30 AM - Depart
Drive through Topolia Gorge. Stop at Agia Sophia cave church. Optional detour: Botanical Garden of Crete (opens 9 AM).
9:00 AM - Elafonisi
Pink-sand lagoon. Wade across to the island side for quieter stretches.
Optional - Kedrodasos
Kedrodasos Beach: 5-min detour on the way back. Wild, undeveloped, great snorkeling.
Dinner
Astrea Garden: Greek-fusion small plates. Book ahead.
August: Arrive by 9 AM. Parking fills and the beach gets very crowded by 11.
05Fri Aug 7
iconic lagoonsunset1h west
7:00 AM - Balos
Drive to Kissamos (~1h). Option A (Drive + Hike): Continue to Balos parking (rough dirt road, ~30 min). Hike down ~20 min. Option B (Boat): Morning boat from Kissamos port (~10:30, returns ~18:00), stops at Gramvousa Island fortress + Balos.
Lunch
Thymari Tavern or Taverna Spilios - both near Falasarna, traditional Cretan tavernas with honest home cooking. Gramboussa Restaurant near Kissamos is another option on the way.
Afternoon - Falasarna
Falasarna Beach: wide golden sand, beach bars. Famous sunset spot.
Dinner
Canale: upscale Mediterranean, cocktails. Or Carte Postale.
Balos by car: Dirt road is bumpy. Arrive early.
06Sat Aug 8
move dayfreshwater lakeold town
Morning
Breakfast at Pallas rooftop. Swim at Nea Chora Beach (10 min walk).
Midday - Lake Kournas
Detour to Lake Kournas: Crete's only freshwater lake. Pedal boats, swimming. Stop at Visalo Ceramics & Cafe nearby.
Afternoon
Check in to Rethymno. Walk the Old Town: Venetian and Ottoman lanes, the Rimondi Fountain.
Sunset
Walk the Fortezza: Venetian fortress, panoramic views. €5 entry.
Dinner
Avli: upscale Cretan in a Venetian courtyard. Book ahead.
07Sun Aug 9
palm beachgorge50 min south
Morning
Drive south through the Kourtaliotiko Gorge. Griffon vultures nest here.
Mid-morning
Preveli Beach: hike down 20–30 min from the parking. Palm-lined river meets the Libyan Sea.
Afternoon
Stop at Kato Moni Preveli: the lower Preveli Monastery with sea views. Optional swim at Plakias.
Dinner
Prima Plora: organic restaurant and wine bar on the water. Book ahead.
08Mon Aug 10
south coast gemssunset spot1h 10m south
Morning
Drive south to Triopetra Beach (~1h 10min from Rethymno). Three rock formations, two stretches of sand.
Midday
Drive 15 min east to Agios Pavlos Sandhills: sand dunes tumbling down to the Libyan Sea.
Lunch
Fish taverna in Agios Pavlos village or Triopetra.
Dinner
The Olive, Wine & Cook House: Cretan-Mediterranean in Rethymno Old Town. Or To Pigadi: hidden courtyard restaurant.
09Tue Aug 11
monasteryeasy beachfarewell dinner
Morning
Arkadi Monastery (~25 min from Rethymno). Venetian Baroque facade, small museum. €4 entry, about 1 hour.
Afternoon
Head to Episkopi Beach: 15 min west of Rethymno. Long golden sand, shallow water, mountain backdrop.
Shopping
Olive oil, honey, raki, herbs, Cretan cheese: Ethnikis Antistaseos street.
Sunset
Fortezza again, or drinks at Mikro Cafe in the Old Town.
Farewell Dinner
Sella Vineria: wine-focused, creative small plates. Or Asikiko: modern Cretan in the Old Town.
10Wed Aug 12
1h 15m to airport
Morning
Check out. Drive to Heraklion Airport (~1h 15min). Allow 2.5 hours before your flight for car return and airport.
If your flight is late
Stop in Heraklion for a final coffee and bougatsa. Koules Fortress at the old harbor is worth a quick look - 5 km from the airport.

Restaurant Guide: Chania

5 evenings (Aug 3–7). Mix of picks.

Tamam Book ahead
Night 1 (Aug 3). Persian-Greek fusion, cozy Old Town alley. Complimentary baklava.
Thalassino Ageri Book ahead
Night 2 (Aug 4). Waterfront seafood taverna at the edge of the Old Town - one of the best in Crete for fresh fish.
Fourogatos Book ahead
Night 3 (Aug 5). Waterfront Cretan cuisine, homemade feta, seafood saganaki.
Astrea Garden Book ahead
Night 4 (Aug 6). Creative small plates, romantic garden, wine pairings.
Canale Walk-in OK
Night 5 (Aug 7). Upscale harbor-front, cocktails, DJ vibes. Reserve for waterfront table.
Carte Postale Walk-in OK
Alternative for any evening - lovely spot in Chania.
Pallas Walk-in OK
Best for breakfast or sunset drinks. Rooftop harbor views.
Kapari Walk-in OK
Lunch spot. Classic taverna, daily specials, great value.

Day-trip lunches (west coast): Thymari Tavern and Taverna Spilios near Falasarna, Gramboussa Restaurant near Kissamos. Taverna Stomio on the Elafonisi road.
