01Arrival, Jumbo & Chania Old TownMon Aug 3
2h drivejumbo stopdinner
Arrival
Land at Heraklion and pick up the rental car.
Jumbo — Heraklion
Stop at Jumbo on 62 Martyron Avenue — on your route west, no detour. Open Mon–Sat 9–21. Beach umbrella, sunscreen, water supplies, snorkeling gear.
Drive West
Highway to Chania (~2 hours). Scenic north-coast drive with mountain views.
Late Afternoon
Check in and walk Chania's Venetian Harbor — the Egyptian Lighthouse, colorful waterfront, then into the Old Town lanes. Zambeliou and Theotokopoulou streets are the most atmospheric.
Dinner
Tamam — your favourite. Cozy Old Town alley, Persian-Greek fusion, complimentary baklava. Book ahead.
02Seitan Limania & ChaniaTue Aug 4
hidden coveseafood dinnerold town

Lighter day — save the big west-coast drives for when your friend arrives.

Morning
Drive 20 min to Seitan Limania on the Akrotiri Peninsula. Dramatic turquoise cove between steep cliffs. Steep 15–20 min hike down — proper shoes, go before 9 AM. Small beach, arrives early or it's packed.
Afternoon
Back to Chania. Explore the Agora (Municipal Market), leather shops on Stivanadika street, the Mosque of the Janissaries. Lunch at Kapari — classic taverna, generous daily specials, great value. Walk-in fine.
Sunset
Drinks at Pallas rooftop — best sunset-over-harbor view in Chania.
Dinner
Thalassino Ageri — from your list! Right on the water at the edge of the Old Town. One of the best seafood tavernas in Crete, famous for fresh fish and sea views. Book ahead.
03Knossos Palace & Airport PickupWed Aug 5
knossos2h to heraklionmuseum
Timing: Flight lands ~12:00. No early alarm needed — leave Chania ~10 AM, pick up your friend, then do Knossos and the museum together in the afternoon.
10:00 AM — Depart Chania
Relaxed morning. Coffee in the Old Town, then hit the highway east (~2 hours to Heraklion).
~12:00 — Airport Pickup
Collect your friend at Heraklion Airport. The airport is small and easy to navigate.
12:30 PM — Lunch in Heraklion
First meal together. Head to Lions Square or the 1866 Market Street for a proper Cretan lunch. Don't skip bougatsa (custard pastry) — Heraklion's signature.
2:30 PM — Archaeological Museum
Start indoors where there's air conditioning. The original Minoan frescoes (Bull Leaper, Ladies in Blue) are here, not at the palace. Combo ticket available. ~1 hour. Seeing this first makes the palace visit much richer.
4:00 PM — Knossos Palace
Only 10 min drive from the museum. By 4 PM the worst heat has passed and the big tour groups have left — this is actually the best time to visit. Book a 4 PM timed entry slot. Budget 1.5 hours. Still bring hat and water.
~6:00 PM — Drive Back
Drive back to Chania (~2h). Quick coffee stop at the Rethymno Venetian Harbor if you feel like stretching your legs — you'll be staying there later in the trip.
Dinner
Fourogatos — waterfront Cretan cuisine, homemade feta, seafood saganaki. Perfect "welcome to Crete" dinner. Book ahead.
04Elafonisi BeachThu Aug 6
pink sand1h 20m each way

Your friend's first full day — taking them to one of Europe's most beautiful beaches.

7:30 AM — Depart
Drive through Topolia Gorge. Stop at the Agia Sophia cave church (short walk up steps). Optional detour: the Botanical Garden of Crete is near the road to Omalos if you want a morning break (opens 9 AM).
9:00 AM — Elafonisi
Wade across the shallow lagoon to the island side for quieter pink-sand stretches. West side of the island is usually less crowded. Warm, shallow, Caribbean-like water. Sunbeds available but bring your own supplies too.
Optional — Kedrodasos
Kedrodasos Beach is a 5-min detour on the way back — wild, undeveloped, backed by juniper trees, great snorkeling. No facilities at all.
Dinner
Astrea Garden — creative Greek-fusion small plates in a romantic garden setting, excellent wine pairings. Book ahead.
August: Arrive by 9 AM. Parking fills and the beach gets very crowded by 11.
05Balos Lagoon & Falasarna SunsetFri Aug 7
iconic lagoonsunset1h west
7:00 AM — Balos
Drive to Kissamos (~1h). Option A — Drive + Hike: Continue to Balos parking (rough dirt road, ~30 min). Hike down ~20 min. Bring lots of water. Option B — Boat: Morning boat from Kissamos port (~10:30, returns ~18:00), stops at Gramvousa Island fortress + Balos.
Lunch
Thymari Tavern (from your list) or Taverna Spilios — both near Falasarna, traditional Cretan tavernas with honest home cooking. Gramboussa Restaurant near Kissamos is another option on the way.
Afternoon — Falasarna
Falasarna Beach — wide golden sand, beach bars. Stay for the sunset — Falasarna is famous across Crete for having the best one. The sky goes orange-to-pink across the open western sea.
Dinner
Canale on the harbor — upscale Mediterranean, cocktails, DJ vibes. Or Carte Postale (from your list) for a more intimate Chania dinner.
Balos by car: Dirt road is bumpy. Park early. Hike is exposed and hot — go before 9 AM.
06Chania → Rethymno via Lake KournasSat Aug 8
move dayfreshwater lakeold town
Morning
Sleep in. Breakfast at Pallas rooftop. Last Chania swim at Nea Chora Beach (10 min walk, fish tavernas around it).
Midday — Lake Kournas
Check out, drive toward Rethymno. Detour to Lake Kournas — Crete's only freshwater lake, set below mountains. Pedal boats, swimming, look for turtles. Stop at Visalo Ceramics & Cafe (from your list) nearby — handmade pottery and good coffee in a garden setting.
Afternoon
Check in to Rethymno. Walk the Old Town — Venetian and Ottoman lanes, the Rimondi Fountain, tiny shops. Smaller and quieter than Chania but equally charming.
Sunset
Walk the Fortezza — massive Venetian fortress on the headland, panoramic views. €5 entry. Stunning at golden hour.
Dinner
Avli (from your list) — upscale Cretan restaurant in a Venetian courtyard with a renowned wine cellar. Book ahead.
07Preveli Beach & Kourtaliotiko GorgeSun Aug 9
palm beachgorge50 min south
Morning
Drive south through the Kourtaliotiko Gorge (from your list) — stop at viewpoints to look down the towering canyon walls. Griffon vultures nest here.
Mid-morning
Preveli Beach — park above, hike down 20–30 min (steep!). A palm-lined river meets the Libyan Sea. Swim in both the warm sea and cool river mouth. No facilities — bring food and water.
Afternoon
Stop at Kato Moni Preveli (from your list) — the lower Preveli Monastery, beautifully perched with sea views. Then optionally swing through Plakias for a late swim and a fish taverna on the sand.
Dinner
Prima Plora (from your list) — organic restaurant and wine bar right on the water in Rethymno. Cretan ingredients, local wines, sunset terrace. Book ahead.
Preveli hike: Walk back up is steep and hot. Wear good shoes, bring more water than you think.
08Triopetra & Agios Pavlos SandhillsMon Aug 10
south coast gemssunset spot1h 10m south

The south coast's hidden treasures — both from your saved list. Far less crowded than the famous beaches, and wilder.

Morning
Drive south to Triopetra Beach (~1h 10min from Rethymno). Named after three dramatic rock formations jutting out of the sea. Two stretches of sand: the western "Big Triopetra" is more open, the eastern side is sheltered and quieter. Turquoise water, barely any tourists compared to the north coast.
Midday
Drive 15 min east to Agios Pavlos Sandhills (from your list) — surreal sand dunes tumbling down to the Libyan Sea. Feels like another planet. Great for photos and swimming. Very little shade, so use your Jumbo umbrella.
Lunch
Simple fish taverna in Agios Pavlos village or back at Triopetra — both have a couple of honest beachfront spots.
Dinner
The Olive, Wine & Cook House (from your list) — creative Cretan-Mediterranean in Rethymno Old Town. Or To Pigadi (from your list) — hidden courtyard restaurant, traditional Cretan menu.
09Arkadi, Episkopi Beach & FarewellTue Aug 11
monasteryeasy beachfarewell dinner

Last full day. Mix culture and relaxation.

Morning
Arkadi Monastery (~25 min from Rethymno). Crete's most significant monastery — central to the 1866 rebellion. Venetian Baroque facade, small museum. €4 entry, about 1 hour. Continue to Margarites village (10 min) — pottery workshops, handmade ceramics.
Afternoon
Head to Episkopi Beach (from your list) — 15 min west of Rethymno. Long golden sand, shallow water, mountain backdrop, far fewer crowds than the town beach. Perfect low-key last afternoon.
Shopping
Olive oil, local honey, raki, herbs, Cretan cheese — Ethnikis Antistaseos street in Old Town has good selections.
Sunset
Fortezza again, or drinks at Mikro Cafe (from your list) in the Old Town.
Farewell Dinner
Sella Vineria (from your list) — wine-focused, intimate, creative small plates. Or Asikiko (from your list) — well-regarded modern Cretan in the Old Town. Either is a great send-off.

Practical Tips

Knossos tickets: Book timed entry online now. €20 adult, combo with the Archaeological Museum available. Free for EU citizens under 25.
August heat: 30–35°C daily. Beach hikes (Balos, Preveli, Seitan Limania) are all exposed and steep. Go early, 2+ liters of water per person, hat, real shoes.
Gas stations: Most close by 7 PM. Highway stations stay open later. Keep the tank half full heading to remote western/southern beaches.
Beaches — what to bring: Buy a beach umbrella at Jumbo (~€15). Sunbed rental at organized beaches is €8–12 for a pair. Triopetra, Agios Pavlos, and Kedrodasos have no facilities — bring everything.
South coast beaches: The Libyan Sea side is noticeably less windy than the north coast in August. Water is a touch warmer too. Triopetra and Agios Pavlos are far less crowded than Elafonisi or Balos.
Rethymno sea turtles: Beaches east of Rethymno are loggerhead turtle nesting grounds (May–Sep). Don't disturb cordoned nests; avoid beach flashlights at night.
Your saved picks I skipped: Gavdos (full-day ferry commitment), Samaria Gorge (full-day hike, logistics complex with one car), Matala/Red Beach/Kommos (reachable but ~2h from Rethymno — doable as a swap for the Triopetra day if you prefer). Loutro (boat-access village from Hora Sfakion — beautiful but adds a long day). All worth it on a longer trip.
